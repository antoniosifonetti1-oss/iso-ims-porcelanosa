Decision brief · Integrated management systems (9001 / 14001 / 27001 / 42001)

Should Porcelanosa run quality, environment, energy, security and AI under one management system?

An integrated management system (IMS) is one set of policies, risk registers, processes, audits and management reviews that serves several ISO standards at once, instead of one separate system per certificate.

It works because ISO management-system standards share the same skeleton (the "harmonized structure", clauses 4 to 10). Only a few requirements are specific to each standard. Integrating means managing the shared part once and the specific part where it belongs.

ADOPT · IN THREE PHASES 1) Merge ISO 9001, 14001 and 50001 into one group-wide system while the 2026 editions force a transition anyway. 2) Add ISO 27001 within 24 months, starting with central IT, e-commerce and the Castellón plants. 3) Run ISO 42001 as a pilot without certification, on the AI configurator and process AI tools.

The catch: year 1 costs about €1.9–3.1 M but audit savings are only about €50 k a year. The value is protecting continuity and customer trust, not efficiency.

The standards in 2026

Four standards, four different jobs and four different maturity levels

Capability vs. hype. A certificate proves that a management system exists and is audited; it does not prove that a product, plant or AI model is good. In particular, ISO 42001 certification is not compliance with the EU AI Act: the standard is a management framework, the law sets legal obligations. It helps to organise the evidence, nothing more.

Reference organisation

Porcelanosa: a Castellón-based ceramics group, export-driven, with 7 brands and 200+ stores

Hypotheses, not facts. Porcelanosa does not publish its internal set-up. We assume that each brand runs its own systems, and that there is no group-wide ISO 27001 (we found no public trace of one). Our conclusions hold even if some of these certificates already exist: the main gain is integration, governance and a single owner for AI risk.
