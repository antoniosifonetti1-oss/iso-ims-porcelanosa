Briefing for managers · ISO 9001 / 14001 / 50001 / 27001 / 42001

Should Porcelanosa Grupo run its ISO standards as one system?

Yes: one group system now, information security in phases, and AI as a pilot.

Team FARA · Built for SM&IT

Our recommendation

Adopt

One group IMS that merges the existing ISO 9001, 14001 and 50001, and feeds CSRD climate reporting.

Adopt, phased

ISO 27001: IT, ERP and e-commerce first, then plants and stores.

Pilot

ISO 42001 for industrial AI and the customer configurator, with a certification decision at month 12.

Audit savings a year: small, so not the reason to do it
The honest catch: about €0.9 M a year buys resilience that is invisible until an incident. The case rests mainly on cyber resilience and on regulation (CSRD), not on efficiency.

    Hidden costs: staff time for awareness training (5,000 × 2 h × €28 ≈ €280 k in year 1); negotiation between the brand companies over one shared system; OT work only during planned kiln stops; overlapping assurance work (CSRD limited assurance, ISO audits, AEO, customer audits) when no single data owner exists; consultant lock-in; and the risk of a “paper system”.

    Break-even calculator

    Two ways to read the cost: how much of it insurance alone repays (fewer and shorter stoppages), and how many sales per year the certificates must protect to cover it.

    More assumptions (all adjustable)
    Margin lost in one major stoppage
    of that cost repaid by insurance alone (expected value)

    Revenue per day of full stoppage = €863 M ÷ 330 days ≈ €2.6 M, an upper bound. Ignores recovery costs, ransom and fines.

    How we scaled the cost (calculation notes)
    • Set-up: €380 k + €430 k + €900 k + €90 k = €1.8 M = 0.21% of 2025 revenue (€863 M) = 1.8% of 2025 capex (€102 M).
    • Running: −€20 k − €22.5 k + €70 k + €250 k + €560 k + €60 k = €897.5 k ≈ 0.9% of EBITDA (>€100 M).
    • Audit saving: 15% of about €150 k of combined fees (A); the IAF MD 11 cap is 20% [16].
    • Scaling: a single 250-employee firm’s 27001 project costs about €85 k [17]. A group 20× larger, with OT and retail, is not linear. Our 27001 programme cost (€430 k, excluding technical controls) assumes about 5× because of shared group processes (A).
    • If 42001 certification follows: +€60 k set-up and +€25 k a year (decision at month 12).

          Learn & quiz

          An IMS runs several ISO standards as one system, because they share the Harmonized Structure

          An integrated management system is an organisational information system: shared processes, documents, risk registers, KPIs and audit records, usually held on one GRC/IMS platform linked to the ERP. Clauses 4 to 10 are the same for all standards; only clause 8 differs.

          Shared onceStandard-specificISO 50001 plugs into the same structure.

          Status in November 2026

          Hype to discount: (a) integration cuts combined audit time by at most 20% [16]; (b) ISO 42001 does not give AI Act conformity, a role that belongs to EN 18286 [11][12]; (c) ISO 9001 and 14001 do nothing for cyber security.
